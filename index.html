Day-to-day care

Maintaining Your Unit

Garbage Disposals

Food scraps, bones, popcorn, peels, and other hard items should never go into a disposal. Only soft, liquid, and/or biodegradable foods should be inserted.

Clear drains are key to a well-functioning home. Avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, and hair down sinks and showers. Simple habits like routine hot-water rinses help prevent buildup and extend the life of your plumbing.

Full disposal care guide

Fridges & Freezers

Refrigerators and freezers must not be overfilled. Overstuffing causes food spoilage and can lead to unit failure. Leave space toward the back of the freezer for air circulation.

Door Locks & Codes

When the latch on the inside of your bedroom door is vertical, the door stays unlocked. When it is horizontal, the door stays locked and your code is needed.

Entering your code incorrectly 3 times will temporarily lock you out. More on access cards and codes.

Smoke Alarms

Smoke alarms chirp when the battery needs to be replaced. You do not need to remove the unit to change the battery; the compartment is on the side of the unit.

Smoke alarms may never be covered or removed. Tampering with a smoke detector will result in a $500 fine.

Electrical & Breaker: how to find and reset a tripped breaker

If power goes out in part of your unit, a circuit breaker may have tripped.

  1. Locate the breaker panel. It is typically a gray metal box in the common hallway outside of the unit. Units and rooms are labeled.
  2. Open the panel door. Inside, you will see a row of switches.
  3. Identify the tripped breaker. The switch will usually sit in the middle or slightly off from the others (not fully in the ON position).
  4. Reset the breaker. Firmly flip the switch all the way to OFF first, then back to ON.

If the breaker trips again right away, stop and submit a maintenance request. It may mean an overload or an electrical issue.

Protecting your unit

Kitchen Disposal Use & Care

Improper use of the kitchen disposal, sink drains, and toilets can cause serious plumbing damage and costly repairs. Residents are responsible for using these systems properly. The disposal is designed to handle small amounts of soft food waste only, not bulk food scraps.

Keep it working

  • Scrape food scraps from plates into the garbage before rinsing.
  • During food prep, place most food waste directly into the garbage.
  • Never put paper, plastic, metal, or any non-food items in the disposal.

Never put these in the disposal:

  • Meat scraps or bones
  • Popcorn
  • Raw hard vegetables
  • Fruit rinds
  • Potato peels
  • Whole food items

Proper disposal operation

  1. Make sure the disposal is clear of debris before use.
  2. Do not pack food into the disposal.
  3. Turn on cold water and let it run.
  4. Flip the disposal switch to process small amounts of food waste.
  5. Let the water run briefly after turning the disposal off to flush the drain.

Important reminder

Disposals are meant to handle minimal leftover food waste. They do not replace the garbage can.

Improper use may result in maintenance charges if damage occurs due to misuse.

Keep it clean

Garbage & Recycling

Using the trash compactor (trash only)

  1. Before opening the door, press 1-2-3-4-* on the keypad.
  2. Wait. Let the compactor cycle through so the arm is in the right position. Do not press the red emergency stop button.
  3. When the first cycle finishes, lift the door latch and slide it RIGHT. The door will unlock.
  4. Open the door and place your bags in the lower compartment.
  5. Close the door and slide the handle LEFT into the latched position. The compactor will start and run a full cycle on its own.
  6. Check that no trash is left outside the compactor.

To report a problem with the compactor, submit a maintenance request in StarRez or email residents@lonemountainhousing.com.

Cardboard recycling

Until a cardboard dumpster is on site (east of Building 99), break down your cardboard and place it in the compactor.

A Cardboard Recycling Bin is also next to the compactor. Please flatten all cardboard boxes before placing them in the bin.

Please do not place garbage in the Recycling Bin. Help keep the property clean and litter-free.

Additional Information

Thermostats

All thermostats are adjustable. If left untouched, they reset to 65 degrees after 16 hours. If a thermostat is unchanged after 48 hours, it resets to 55 degrees.

Cove heating takes time, possibly a couple of hours to go from 55 degrees to a comfortable 70. Turning the thermostat higher will not heat the room any faster. To keep the heat consistent, check your thermostat regularly.

Gym

Workout room hours: 6 AM – 10 PM. Your room key will get you access.

Closets

Closets are lockable with a standard padlock. Padlocks are not provided by management and must be purchased by the tenant.

Mattresses

Vehicle registration

When a registered vehicle can still be ticketed or towed

Registered resident vehicles will not be ticketed or towed unless the vehicle:

  • Is parked in a space reserved for disability parking only
  • Is parked in a no parking zone
  • Is parked in a fire lane
  • Is blocking access to the trash receptacles or dumpsters
  • Is clearly disabled and has broken parts, glass, or flat tires

Penalties

  • First offense: warning
  • Second offense and all offenses after: $25.00 per offense

Any repeat offense, more than three (3) ticketed offenses in any one week, or more than ten (10) ticketed offenses in any consecutive eight (8) week period, will result in immediate disablement of the vehicle by booting or other appropriate means and/or towing and impoundment. The fine for release of any booted or impounded vehicle is $500.00 per offense. The property manager may also determine other special purposes from time to time.

All offenses are evidenced by a ticket issued by an authorized Property Manager. The fine is due and payable, in cash, at the office of Property Management within five (5) days after the ticket is issued. A record of all offenses, including the name and address of the offender, the license plate number of the vehicle involved, sanctions levied, and fines paid, is kept and maintained by Lone Mountain Property Management.
